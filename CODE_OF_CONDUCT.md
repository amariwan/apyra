# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Apyra welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Everyone taking part in project spaces is expected to help keep them respectful, constructive, and
safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate when discussing ideas, code, and people.
- Give and receive feedback constructively; focus criticism on the work.
- Assume good intent while addressing the impact of someone's words or actions.
- Respect differing viewpoints and make room for others to participate.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or attention without consent.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or repeatedly ignoring reasonable requests to stop.
- Any other conduct that would make participation hostile or unsafe.

This policy applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and project
events, as well as when someone is representing Apyra publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a conduct concern privately to the project maintainer using the contact information on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/amariwan). Do not report conduct concerns in a
public issue. Include what happened, where it happened, and any context that will help the
maintainer review it. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

The maintainer will review reports and may take proportionate action, including a private warning,
temporary restrictions, or removal from project spaces. Anyone affected by an enforcement decision
may contact the maintainer privately to request a review.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
adapted for this project's size and contact channels.
